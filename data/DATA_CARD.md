# Data card
All cases, organizations, payment identifiers, notes and procedures in this repository are hand-authored fictional examples for a personal prototype. They do not represent any real bank or its policies. No external dataset was used. The development examples and expected outcomes are not a held-out evaluation set. Do not use real payment or customer data. Synthetic examples are too limited to establish real-world accuracy or safety.

The Northstar and Harbor partners, response messages, and partner-specific procedures are invented. Comparison cases are development examples authored alongside the implementation, not independent held-out evidence.

`evaluations/comparison_cases.json` has 32 fictional development cases covering missing and invalid details, unknown codes, partner mismatches, matching records, resolved and historical issues, and injection attempts. `evaluations/candidate_cases.json` contains 10 separately stored candidate cases. Both sets and labels were authored during development by the same assistant; separation does not make them independent. Have a person independently review categories, required evidence and acceptable actions, check for overlapping or near-duplicate wording, and reserve an untouched set before claiming held-out results. No real-world rates, bank policies, or outcome probabilities are represented. Unsupported scenarios are labeled for human review rather than treated as a simulated resolution.
