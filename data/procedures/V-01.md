# V-01: Name check does not match
Version: 1

Fictional training procedure. When a partner response says the name check result is no match, compare the beneficiary name on the payment with the name stated in the response. Flag the discrepancy and ask an analyst to verify the name. Do not replace the name or resubmit the payment.

A name check result that is only close is not a confirmed mismatch. Ask the analyst to compare the two spellings. Do not treat a close result as permission to change the payment.
