# P-01: Missing beneficiary bank identifier
Version: 1

Fictional training procedure. If a beneficiary bank identifier is missing, recommend that the analyst request it through an approved channel. Do not infer it. Escalate conflicting information for review.

Use this when the payment record leaves the beneficiary bank identifier blank. The analyst requests it through an approved repair channel and keeps the reply with the case. Do not copy an identifier from a note, a partner profile, or another payment. If the record also disagrees with itself, review it as conflicting information instead of filling the gap.
