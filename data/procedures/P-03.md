# P-03: Conflicting case information
Version: 1

Fictional training procedure. If a note conflicts with a payment record, flag the discrepancy and recommend analyst verification. Do not choose a value without evidence.

Use this when the case itself reports a conflict, including a note that names a different beneficiary, bank, amount, or reference than the payment record. Show both values to the analyst. Do not pick one, repair the payment, or treat a partner procedure as permission to overwrite the record.
