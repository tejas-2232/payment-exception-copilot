# F-01: Fictional fraud-review guidance
Version: 1

Fictional training guidance F-01: Review the supplied history and any recent changes. Verify flagged facts and contextual explanations with an analyst. A signal or priority score is not a fraud finding. Do not block, release, alter, or send a payment; missing or inconsistent information requires review.

The score orders the fictional queue only. Recently added beneficiary is 25 points when the beneficiary date is within 30 days of the review timestamp. A payment-instruction change within 24 hours is 35 points. An amount above three times the average of at least three same-currency prior payments is 40 points. Zero means none of those checks fired. It does not mean the payment is safe. Bands are routine at 0, watch below 40, elevated from 40 through 74, and priority at 75 or above. An analyst still decides what to do.
